Project services and scope of work
ServiceWhat the work covers
Matter-specific consultationReview the relevant facts and information, advise on the matter and provide a written summary.
Contract reviewReview contract terms, identify risks and disadvantages, and propose amendments.
Contract draftingEstablish the business terms, prepare a draft and explain the key provisions.
Standard demand or notice letterPrepare a standard demand or notice letter from the facts and documents received.
Written legal opinionStudy the facts, documents and legal issues, and prepare a written opinion.
Negotiation or meeting attendancePrepare issues and attend within scope; agree the duration and decision-making authority in advance.
Company incorporationPrepare and coordinate incorporation documents for the agreed information and structure.
Changes to company registrationPrepare and submit changes such as directors, signing authority, registered address or other agreed particulars.
LitigationAssess facts, evidence and options; prepare pleadings and conduct the agreed phase of proceedings.
Judgment enforcementReview entitlement documents and asset information; pursue attachment, seizure and follow-up within the agreed scope.
Project or fixed-scope engagementScope a project such as PDPA review, debt review, HR and employment, BOI, licensing or training, with defined deliverables.

Fees are determined by the scope of work. Please contact the office for a quotation.

Scope, document volume, language, revisions, timing and exclusions are agreed in the quotation. Litigation and enforcement identify the work phases covered. VAT, government fees and other expenses are stated separately in the quotation.

The working process

Receive the facts and documents → assess the issues → agree scope and quotation → perform and review → hand over and follow up as agreed.

If you have received court papers or a notice, mention the receipt date and any approaching deadline at initial contact.
