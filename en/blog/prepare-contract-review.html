PREPARATION GUIDE / THAILAND

What to prepare before a contract review

A practical checklist for business owners and teams preparing to brief a lawyer.

LAKPPrepared 27 October 2026General information

Use this to organise information. It does not determine rights or legal deadlines in a specific matter.

  1. The decision you need to make

    Explain the purpose: reviewing before signature, considering a counterparty’s draft, or preparing points for negotiation.

  2. The working version

    Gather the current draft, schedules and relevant amendments. Mark the version date and whether it has been signed.

  3. People and responsibilities

    Identify the parties, coordinator and business decision-maker. Counterparty details may be needed for intake checks.

  4. Business terms

    Summarise the goods or services, price, payment stages, delivery and terms already agreed or still under discussion.

  5. Known dates

    List negotiation, signing, delivery and other known dates with their source. This checklist does not determine legal deadlines.

  6. Questions to resolve

    Separate concerns, unclear terms and commercial limits so the review can be scoped around the decision you need.

Start your inquiry with an overview

Do not send all documents or confidential details in the first message. Share the topic, a way to reach you and known dates. The office can then assess what information is needed.
