PREPARATION GUIDE / THAILAND

Organise payment dates before seeking advice

Prepare the agreement, timeline and records connected with a payment matter.

LAKPPrepared 27 October 2026General information

Use this to organise information. It does not determine rights or legal deadlines in a specific matter.

  1. Source documents

    Gather the agreement, payment provisions, schedules and relevant amendments.

  2. A dated timeline

    Record document issue, delivery, acceptance, invoicing and actual payment dates, linking each date to its source.

  3. Amounts and status

    Separate documented amounts, payments received, disputed amounts and information still to be checked.

  4. Communications

    Keep relevant letters, emails and messages in date order without changing the original records.

  5. Questions for assessment

    State the decision you need help with and any known deadline. Whether a default or legal entitlement arises requires case-specific consideration of the facts and law.

Start your inquiry with an overview

Do not send all documents or confidential details in the first message. Share the topic, a way to reach you and known dates. The office can then assess what information is needed.
