PREPARATION GUIDE / THAILAND

How to start working with LAKP

From your first inquiry to scope assessment and an agreed engagement.

LAKPPrepared 27 October 2026General information

Use this to organise information. It does not determine rights or legal deadlines in a specific matter.

  1. Select the appropriate service

    Project addresses defined matters, Retainer provides continuing support, and Setup establishes legal priorities and working arrangements. If the appropriate service is not yet clear, begin with a brief outline of the situation.

  2. Start with essential information

    Share a short topic, contact details and known deadlines. Do not include confidential documents or a detailed case history in the first message.

  3. Initial assessment

    The office considers fit, information needed and responsibility. Clicking a button or sending a message does not confirm an engagement.

  4. Agree the scope

    Before work starts, agree deliverables, language, document volume, revisions, timing, fees and exclusions.

  5. For an approaching deadline

    Contact the office directly and mention the date. You do not need to download a guide or start with Setup. An inquiry does not guarantee the office can act in time.

Start your inquiry with an overview

Do not send all documents or confidential details in the first message. Share the topic, a way to reach you and known dates. The office can then assess what information is needed.
